# Development Workflow

Diamond Ace Construction began as an AI-assisted scaffold and is being rebuilt into a production client website through deliberate review, testing, and incremental design phases.

## Working principles

- Treat AI output as implementation assistance, not final product judgment.
- Keep business claims grounded in services Diamond Ace actually provides.
- Do not invent project photography, testimonials, reviews, certifications, statistics, or portfolio work.
- Stock media can represent a service category but must never be described as a Diamond Ace project.
- Keep business and service information centralized so marketing copy, forms, validation, SEO, and email behavior do not drift apart.
- Prefer small reviewed phases over one giant generated rewrite.
- Run lint, explicit TypeScript checks, production build, and E2E tests before calling a phase stable.

## V2 rebuild phases

1. Foundation, accurate data, homepage hero, service cards, navigation, and test configuration
2. Complete homepage sections and responsive polish
3. Services page redesign
4. Our Approach page redesign
5. Contact and estimate-form redesign
6. Production email and lead-data workflow
7. SEO, accessibility, performance, and test hardening
8. Launch, case study, and professional documentation
